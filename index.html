NEW STUDENT?

How to get started

  1. Create your account. Select “New student? Create an account” and enter your ADSSU student ID, institutional email and academic details.
  2. Confirm your email and sign in. Open the confirmation link sent to your mailbox, then return here and select Scholar Login.
  3. Wait for staff assignment. Staff reviews your registration and assigns Excellence Scholar or Access Scholar. Grade submission becomes available after assignment.
  4. Submit and track. Submit your academic summary and required evidence, follow verification, and view your compliance result and renewal decision.

Already have an assigned account? Use Scholar Login. Creating an account does not guarantee a scholarship award. The scholarship directory is for browsing; it does not accept applications to the listed programs.
